# Voice profiles — the two-account model

Organic runs on **two accounts in two registers** (see
`docs/wiki/marketing/organic-social-playbook.md` §2A). Every draft routes to exactly one
profile. **Default: strict-scientific.**

| Profile | Account | Who | Voice | Citation gate |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **strict-scientific** | @TheSynthesisLab | Anton (chemist) | Honest-skeptic synthesis chemist — substance, mechanism, proof-of-work | **APPLIES** — every study/author/year/result must be verified |
| **personified** | brother's handle | brother (experiential) | First-person n=1, warm, relatable, community | **N/A** — this voice never makes a claim that could need a citation |

## Profile: strict-scientific (default)

The existing chemist voice — the moat. Full spec in
[voice-and-voc.md](voice-and-voc.md); the compliance + citation gate in
[compliance.md](compliance.md) applies in full. Use for anything on **@TheSynthesisLab**:
mechanism answers, "a chemist's take" posts, article→thread recaps, NMR/proof-of-work,
correct-and-add replies. This is the category-authority register. Nothing here changes — the
second profile is added *alongside* it.

## Profile: personified (experiential)

The brother's account. A relatable person running an n=1 self-experiment and talking about
it — not an expert, and never pretending to be one.

| Parameter | Rule |
|---|---|
| Register | Warm, first-person, conversational. Memes, questions, "logging my run" |
| Person | Always first person — *his own* lived experience only |
| Content | What it felt like, what he noticed, what he's curious about, community questions |
| **Claims** | **ZERO factual / mechanism / citation claims. Hard rule.** |
| Citation gate | **N/A** — the voice never cites, because it never asserts a mechanism or study |

**The hard no-claims rule (why this profile exists at all):** the personified account may
**never** explain how ISR/eIF2B works, cite a study/author/year/result, quote a number as
fact, or make a health/therapeutic claim. If a draft on this account starts to sound like an
expert, it has failed — a hollow expert take fails instantly with this audience **and drags
@TheSynthesisLab down with it**, because both accounts point to the same products. Kept
purely experiential, this voice cannot be "wrong," which is exactly its safety.

**Allowed** (experiential framing):
- "Week 3 of the A15 run — here's what the mornings feel like now."
- "Anyone else find the fog lifts *slower* than they expected? Curious what your timeline was."
- "Not a chemist, just logging my own n=1. Take it for what it is."

**Not allowed** (route these to strict-scientific / @TheSynthesisLab instead):
- Anything explaining the mechanism ("the ISR brake works by…").
- Any study, dose-response, or "the data shows…" statement presented as fact.
- Any comparison claim about efficacy vs another compound stated as established.

If a personified draft needs a factual point to stand up, that is a signal the point belongs
on the chemist account — **do not** launder it through the experiential voice.

## Cross-account rule (both profiles)

**No sockpuppet cross-promo.** The two accounts never stage a fake-independent mutual product
endorsement or coordinated reply/upvote rings — that is the astroturfing that bans both
(see [reddit.md](reddit.md) §disclosure, [compliance.md](compliance.md)). Light *genuine*
interaction between them is fine. Never imply the two accounts are unrelated strangers who
independently discovered A15.
