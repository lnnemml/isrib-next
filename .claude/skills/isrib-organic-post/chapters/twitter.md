# X/Twitter — persona, threads, replies, the flywheel

Condensed from `docs/wiki/marketing/organic-social-playbook.md` §4. Wiki is master.

## Persona & posture

"The Synthesis Lab" — pseudonymous chemist writing about the ISR pathway, cellular stress,
and cognition. Bio links the journal. **X is vendor-tolerant**, so the A15 association is
open here — journal links carry freely, no per-post disclosure needed (the persona is openly
A15-associated). The algorithm rewards **consistency + reply engagement**, not volume.

## Content pillars

1. **Science thread, 1×/week** — the main output. Repurpose one journal article.
2. **Honest-skeptic take** — *"Everyone's hyping [compound]. Here's what the data actually
   shows."* Contrarian-but-rigorous. Banned words still apply.
3. **Reply engagement, daily (10–15 min)** — reply to longevity/nootropics/biohacker
   accounts with genuine chemist insight. This is how a small account grows: ride bigger
   accounts' reach with replies worth reading. Target modafinil/brain-fog/longevity/eIF2
   conversations.
4. **Transparency signal, occasional** — *"Ran NMR on a fresh batch — here's the trace."*
   Proof-of-chemist (no doxxing).

## Article → thread recipe (6–10 posts)

1. **Hook post** — mirror the reader's pain or a counterintuitive claim from the article.
   No link yet. (Pull the hook logic from `boron-letters` / `breakthrough-advertising`.)
2. **Reframe** — the ISR as a biological brake, not a willpower problem.
3–7. **One idea per post** — walk the article's spine: mechanism (eIF2B), the key study
   (name it plainly), what it means, the honest limit ("mouse data; human data is limited").
   Keep each post standalone-quotable.
8. **Honest caveat post** — the skeptic beat. This is the trust asset; never skip it.
9. **Payoff + link** — the one-line takeaway → link the full journal article
   (`utm_source=twitter&utm_medium=post`).

Thread rules: no banned words; one idea per post; the caveat is mandatory; the link goes
**last**, never first.

## The flywheel

Threads → profile visits → journal → `/products/isrib-a15`. Threads quote-tweeted by
accounts >10k → editorial backlinks → **faster SEO ramp on the young journal domain.** The
backlink is the real prize; treat impressions/followers as leading indicators only.

## Reply craft

- Add a fact the original poster didn't have (chemistry/mechanism/what a paper actually says).
- Never argue; correct-and-add. Honest-skeptic, not know-it-all.
- Link only if the article directly deepens the point — otherwise just be useful.

## Compliance on X

Same hard gate (compliance.md). Naming modafinil/racetams/Adderall **educationally** is fine
here (not paid Meta creative). Still: no medical/dementia/cancer/guarantee claims;
research-use framing; honest-skeptic voice.
