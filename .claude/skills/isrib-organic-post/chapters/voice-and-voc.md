# Voice & Voice-of-Customer

Condensed from `docs/wiki/journal/writing-rules.md` (voice) + `marketing/voice-of-customer.md`
+ `product/avatar.md`. Wiki is master.

## The voice (this IS the conversion asset)

Peer-to-peer between two technically literate people. A synthesis chemist talking to a
paper-reading, hype-fatigued biohacker.

| Parameter | Rule |
|---|---|
| Register | Technically precise but accessible |
| Person | First person where there's genuine synthesis/use experience |
| Skepticism | Always present — "Research is promising, human data is limited." |
| Banned words | revolutionary, breakthrough, game-changer, miracle, Limitless (see compliance.md) |
| Use instead | notable, significant, worth examining, the data suggests |

The honest-skeptic voice converts *because* the avatar is jaded. Never trade it for hype.
A confident hedge ("this is interesting but unproven in humans") beats a bold claim every
time with this audience.

## The avatar in one line

Analytically sophisticated biohacker / founder / engineer, ~30–55, burned by modafinil and
nootropic stacks, identifies with their brain as their primary asset, wants results they can
*feel* and *verify*, allergic to marketing. They read papers. Respect that.

## VOC bank — pull real phrases, don't invent

**Before-state (pain):**
- "My ADHD meds stopped working. Turns out my brain had a 'brake' on."
- "cognitive decline I couldn't fix with just rest."
- "foggy no matter how much I sleep."

**After-state (desired):**
- "It wasn't a buzz. It was quiet clarity."
- "I can go 4–5 hours and still feel relatively clear."
- "like going from a blurry photo to 4K."

**Identity / mindset:**
- "I optimize everything — why not cognition?"
- "Mental clarity is a non-negotiable for people who build things."

**Skepticism / hype fatigue (mirror this to build trust):**
- "Most nootropics promise a lot, but I rarely notice anything."
- "I don't want hype. I want results I can feel in 72 hours."

**Metaphors (use consistently):**
- the **biological brake** (central ISR metaphor)
- Ctrl+Alt+Del / mental reset
- defragging a cluttered hard drive · factory reset
- "reversible blockage, not permanent degradation" (the most conversion-relevant claim)

## Usage

Mirror the reader's own before-state language back to them, then reframe with the mechanism.
Never put a VOC after-state quote in a testimonial frame in an organic post unless it's a
real, attributable quote — paraphrase the *sentiment* instead. (Compliance: no fabricated
testimonials.)
