# Beliefs & Objections

Condensed from `docs/wiki/product/beliefs-and-objections.md` (master). A good organic post
**moves exactly one belief** or **answers exactly one objection** — not all of them. Pick
the one the reader's question actually raises.

## The 6 beliefs required to buy (move one per post)

1. **It's biological, not a discipline failure.** Brain fog / memory / motivation loss come
   from a deeper biological block, not laziness. → reframes self-blame into a solvable
   mechanism. *(blog/symptom posts open here.)*
2. **The ISR is the root cause — and it's reversible.** An internal brake on cognition
   under chronic stress/age/burnout, and it's fixable. *(science posts open here.)*
3. **ISRIB A15 is categorically different**, not "another stack" — it reboots protein
   synthesis via eIF2B, not a neurotransmitter nudge. *(compare posts / "I tried everything"
   threads open here — this is the differentiation belief.)*
4. **Real people have used it and seen lasting benefit.** Social proof. *(handle honestly —
   paraphrase sentiment, cite forum reports as reports, never fabricate.)*
5. **It's safe, non-stimulant, no crash/anxiety.** Answers the core fear. *(safety posts.)*
6. **This is my chance to unlock my full cognition — and I deserve it.** The identity belief.
   *(the emotional close; use lightly organically — never salesy.)*

## Objection → honest response (answer one when raised)

| Objection | Honest response posture |
|---|---|
| Is it safe? | Animal safety is notably clean; **human long-term data is limited** — say both. Never overclaim. |
| Is it legal? | Research compound; research-use framing. Don't give legal advice. |
| Will I feel anything? | Subtle, not a stimulant buzz; some feel it in days, some don't — honest variance. |
| I've tried everything | Belief 3 — different mechanism (eIF2B/ISR), not another neurotransmitter nudge. |
| Is it a stimulant? | No — that's the point; contrast with modafinil's forced alertness. |
| Will it make me anxious? | Reports lean the other way (calm/grounded), but frame as reports, not a promise. |
| How do I know it's not placebo? | Point to the mechanism + preclinical data; stay honest about human-data gaps. |
| How do I take it? | Oral, low mg; capsules removed the DMSO problem. (guide territory.) |
| Purity / is it real A15? | In-house synthesis + third-party NMR/COA — the shop's actual differentiator. |

## The differentiation table (Belief 3 — the sales tool against what they already tried)

Neurotransmitter drugs (modafinil, Adderall, racetams, noopept) *push* an existing system
harder. ISRIB A15 *removes a block* on protein synthesis via eIF2B. Different level of
biology → different (subtler, non-stimulant) felt effect. This contrast — stated honestly,
mechanism-first — is what converts the jaded Stage 3–4 reader. Pair with `dr-swipe-file`
comparison structure only if building a landing block; organically, just state the contrast.
