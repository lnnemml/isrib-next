# Marketing — Competitive Landscape

> From Master Intelligence Report (Part 3.4). The positioning gap isrib.shop owns.

## The gap

All current ISRIB A15 vendors are scientific reagent suppliers (AMSBIO, TargetMol,
MyBioSource, Fisher). They write for lab procurement teams — CAS numbers, assay
specs, quote forms — not for the actual end-user. That is the entire opening.

| Dimension | Reagent suppliers | isrib.shop owns |
|---|---|---|
| Language | CAS numbers, molecular weights | Human experience: "quiet clarity", "brain woke up", "4K vs blurry" |
| Audience | Lab technicians, institutions | Biohackers, founders, burnout survivors |
| Trust signal | B2B portals, quote forms | NMR COA, in-house synthesis transparency, chemist credibility |
| Framing | Research chemical, not for human use | Research compound — science explained for people who read papers |
| Price | AMSBIO $415 / 50mg | $200 / 1g — ~20× better value, stated explicitly |
| Preparation | DMSO / animal dosing calculators | Capsules eliminate DMSO — a direct answer to a real objection |
| Content | Specs only | Full scientific education + mechanism + testimonials |
