## DIAGNOSE (Breakthrough Advertising — ch01/02/03)

- **Awareness (ch02): Problem-Aware → Solution-Aware.** They feel the fog and have tried
  prescription stimulants / racetams / stacks, but don't know an ISR-inhibitor *category*
  exists. Headline job = reframe/dramatize the problem, not promise a result. The validated
  **"Your Brain Isn't Broken. It's Stuck."** is a problem reframe (permanent damage →
  fixable state) and is outcome-free → keep it.
- **Sophistication (ch03): Stage 3, tipping into 4.** Raw claims AND competing mechanisms
  (dopaminergic / cholinergic / adaptogen) are exhausted. The move is **lead with a NEW
  MECHANISM — the ISR/eIF2B "biological brake"** — and reverse the arrangement (mechanism in
  the headline/lead, claim in the subhead). The mechanism is new to this market and
  *believable + significant* (UCSF/Walter/Science/eLife/Calico) — the two conditions ch03
  requires. **Key insight: Schwartz's Stage-3 "sell the how, not the what" and our
  compliance guardrail point the SAME way — mechanism-first is both the highest-converting
  and the Meta-safe move.**
- **Dominant desire (ch01): "get my edge back / stop losing it."** Highest urgency ×
  staying power × scope; already burning in the biohacker world (we *channel*, not create).
  Dominant performance to feature = **releasing the brake so baseline cognition returns —
  restoration, not enhancement.** Physical facts (purity, COA, chemist) document it *after*
  the headline, never in it.

## DECISION — lead identity (ratified by Anton, 2026-09-09)

**Stuck-brake high-performer**, NOT "55+ aging-decline." Rationale (ch08 identification:
attach the role the prospect *longs for*, never the one he *fears*): a 58-year-old founder
identifies as a *capped high-performer*, not a *declining patient*. The current draft's
"Your Brain at 55 Isn't Declining" labels them with the identity they flee and narrows the
page to one cohort. One page on the optimizer identity fits a burned-out 35-yo founder AND a
still-sharp 58-yo exec; the 55–64 low-CPA over-index is a **targeting/creative** choice (who
sees the ad), not a reason to write decline copy.
