# Marketing — Messaging Angles

> From Offer Brief + Master Intelligence Report (Parts 3.5, 5) + Landing Redesign
> Summary. Structured copy raw material for landing/product pages and `/go`.

## Primary headline (recommended — hits the core wound)

**"Your Brain Isn't Broken. It's Stuck."** — the strongest validated element from
the current landing; keep it. The biological brake that kills memory under chronic
stress — and the research compound designed to release it.

## Alternative angles

- **The Anti-Stimulant:** Stop treating burnout with stimulants. Why modafinil makes
  it worse — and how eIF2B stabilization actually fixes it.
- **The Mental Reset:** For the brain that's tried everything — the research compound
  that hits the reset button on chronic stress.
- **The 4K Clarity:** From a blurry photo to 4K — clears deep mental fog when rest
  and racetams fail.
- **The Lost Edge:** Get your primary asset back. How high-stakes founders reverse
  cognitive decline at the cellular level.

## Recommended page structure (belief-gate order)

Above-fold hook + subhead + CTA → Agitation (VOC language, name the fog) [Belief 1]
→ Mechanism (ISR/eIF2B in 4 sentences, brake metaphor) [Belief 2] → Differentiation
(neurotransmitter drugs vs. ISR inhibition table) [Belief 3] → Social proof
(specific testimonials with days/hours/context) [Belief 4] → Trust/safety (COA,
synthesis transparency, chemist credibility) [Belief 5] → Identity CTA [Belief 6]
→ Product + CTA → FAQ (hard objections, incl. cancer risk answered honestly).

## Creative compliance (hard rules)

- **Surface scope (Anton, 2026-08-31):** "ad-facing" = the ad creative + ad copy +
  **the page the paid ad lands on**. Meta reviews the destination page, not just the
  creative. So:
  - **Paid-traffic destination** (the A15 landing / any URL a Meta ad points to):
    NO prescription-drug brand names, NO asserted efficacy/health claims, cancer only
    *answered* as an objection. Kept clean.
  - **Organic-only product pages** (Bromantane, N-Acetyl-Bromantane, ZZL-7, MPEP,
    Original — SEO/word-of-mouth, not Meta ad targets): may name related research
    compounds (`Ladasten`, `sydnocarb`, `phenamin`) and carry the legacy mechanism
    copy. Anton owns and accepts this compliance.
- Never name prescription drugs in ad copy (Meta rejects). Use category terms:
  "prescription stimulants," "popular nootropic stacks."
- No dementia claims. **Cancer risk claims never appear** (unsupported; the
  objection is only *answered* in FAQ).
- Don't oversell as "Limitless" — honest, mechanism-anchored framing converts this
  jaded Stage 3–4 audience better.

## Validated learnings (from paid traffic to date)

- 1% Lookalike (buyer list) outperformed broad targeting on checkout→submit rates.
- Direct-to-landing beat prelanding for warm creative (creative already primes).
- Self-contained checkout beat cross-domain (trust break at the isrib-a15.com →
  isrib.shop switch) — **the migration resolves this structurally.**
- Least-favorite creative ("Before Burnout v1") outperformed favorites early —
  trust data over intuition; keep single-variable testing discipline.
