# Product — Avatar

> Synthesized from Avatar Sheet + Master Intelligence Report (Part 1). The complete
> profile of the ISRIB A15 buyer.

## Demographics

- Age 25–45 (paid-traffic data note: the 55–64 cohort showed dramatically lower CPA
  than the originally-targeted 35–44 — demographic data has overridden founder
  intuition before; see analytics summaries).
- ~70% male; growing female interest among high-performing professionals.
- Primary markets: US, UK, Germany, Canada, Australia. Emerging: Poland, Ukraine,
  Czechia.
- Income: $5–20K/mo solopreneurs; $100K+ founders/executives.
- Professions: software engineers, AI researchers, startup founders, exec knowledge
  workers, neuro/biochem PhD students, high-functioning creatives.
- Self-identities: biohackers, longevity enthusiasts, ADHD professionals, elite
  performers, neurodivergent optimizers, underground cognition experimenters.

## Goals

- Short-term: restore clarity/memory in deep work; reduce procrastination and
  cognitive friction; sharpen focus before deadlines.
- Long-term: stay cognitively competitive into 40s–60s; high-performance / low-stress
  lifestyle; be part of the "cutting-edge elite" using science to outpace decline.

## Emotional core

- They identify their brain as their **primary asset** — source of income, status,
  identity.
- They fear becoming mentally irrelevant, slow, or average — losing the "cognitive
  arms race."
- High-agency, self-experimenting, rational — but deeply emotional about losing
  their edge.
- Seek peer validation on Reddit / LongeCity / Discord. Will pay a premium for rare
  compounds if they sense clear ROI in performance or identity.

## Core emotional wound (the single thing copy speaks to)

The terrifying realization that their primary asset — their brain — is failing them
despite doing everything right. Not laziness, not bad habits — something biological
they can't fix with more discipline, sleep, or nootropics. The fear of permanently
losing their edge to burnout.

## Emotional journey to purchase

Awareness (Reddit thread / comparison chart; skeptical-curious) → Frustration
(stacks don't deliver; burned out but ambitious) → Active search (hunts non-stimulant
upgrades; triangulates ISRIB as "different") → Research mode (reads Walter's papers,
forum logs; intellectually convinced but hesitant) → Last straw (a bad week; cost of
NOT acting becomes real; emotionally ready) → Relief & commitment (first trial shows
subtle-but-clear results; becomes loyal, evangelizes).
